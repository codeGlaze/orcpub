Kit Icon Workbench

Each kit keeps its pack icon (backpack or chest) and gets a small badge that says what kind of kit it is. Pick a pack on the left, choose its badge from the icon sheet, then set the corner, size and backing. The row at the top shows all eight together, the way the Kits block lists them. Your picks are kept in this browser; copy the result at the bottom.

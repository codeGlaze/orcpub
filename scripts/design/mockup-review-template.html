Two ways to say "you haven't read this one yet":

2A weapon they are not trained in

As it stands, a closed row shows nothing (2a), so the mastery is easy to miss. Your idea: a thin grey line (2c) with a homebrew switch to use it anyway as a house rule (2d).

3A compact Weapon Mastery panel

Two lines under the Weapons heading. At rest nothing changes; "Change" plans a swap for the next Long Rest in a bottom sheet. Only a Long Rest allows a swap. A Barbarian or Fighter swaps one weapon; a Paladin, Ranger or Rogue can change all.

4The ? opens a card at the bottom

Your pick: a sheet at the bottom, like the roll result, so every card on a phone opens the same way. It closes with the × at the top, like the roll result, so Close never scrolls out of reach.

5Prone opens inside the card

Tapping Prone opens its rule right under the word; tapping it again closes it. One level only. Long text scrolls inside the sheet.

6After a hit, the roll result offers it

A player who never read Topple is told when it applies, with the numbers filled in. Prone opens here the same way.

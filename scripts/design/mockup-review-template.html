Weapon Mastery Mockups

The real Weapons table with the new parts drawn in, at phone size. A fighter trained with the battleaxe (Topple), longsword (Sap) and handaxe (Vex), but not the glaive (Graze).

Open: needs you

  • Spells cast from items (group G): A on the item only, B1 inside the level tables, B2 a From Items table, or A plus B2?

Charges: one pool per item (decided). Rest controls: the layout agent's design; group D is superseded. Wording is parked for one pass at the end.

Decided

  • Band joined to its row; its ? glows a few times until opened. No "New" tag.
  • Untrained weapons get a thin grey line, on by default; a stein switch uses the mastery anyway.
  • On a phone, cards open at the bottom; rules open inside them.
  • Mastery panel: one box, three chips on one line; stein switch for free add and remove.
  • Long Rest asks about masteries only when there is a choice; otherwise a quiet line with Undo.
  • Undo lasts until the character changes, and survives logging off.
  • Rest bar: Dawn, Short Rest, Long Rest. Dawn is never assumed: after a Long Rest without it, the app asks "Did dawn pass?".

Bugs fixed on the way

  • The app's rule card ran off the right edge of a phone.
  • A saved character could not keep a once-per-dawn weapon use.

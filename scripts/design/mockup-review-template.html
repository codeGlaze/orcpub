3A Weapon Mastery panel above the table

Your idea: one row for what is mastered, one for the swap to make at the next Long Rest. Only a Long Rest allows a swap, not a Short Rest. How much can change depends on the class: a Barbarian or Fighter swaps one weapon; a Paladin, Ranger or Rogue can change all of them.

4The ? opens a card at the bottom

Your pick: a sheet at the bottom, like the roll result, so every card on a phone opens the same way. It closes with the × at the top, like the roll result, so Close never scrolls out of reach.

5Prone opens inside the card

Tapping Prone opens its rule right under the word; tapping it again closes it. One level only. Long text scrolls inside the sheet.

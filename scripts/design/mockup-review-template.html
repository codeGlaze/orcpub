1The band, joined to its row

Decided: no New tag (it was missed even when looked for). The ? glows instead, pulsing a few times and then slowing to a stop, like the 1H / 2H switch; opening it once stops it for good.

2A weapon they are not trained in

Decided: the thin grey line shows by default, and a display setting can turn it off. The homebrew switch uses the mastery anyway as a house rule.

3The Weapon Mastery panel, round 2

For your notes: a box sets it apart, the heading is a plain title rather than an amber tag (amber tags stay on the bands), and each mastered weapon is its own chip, with its mastery in small amber letters. At rest it just says "3 of 3". The stein switch lets you add or remove masteries freely as a house rule; the arrow plans a swap.

4The Long Rest asks

The app has no Long Rest pop-up today: the button rests at once. For a character with Weapon Mastery it would open this sheet, with "Keep all" chosen, or the planned swap already filled in. Cancel leaves everything as it was.

5The ? opens a card at the bottom

Your pick: a sheet at the bottom, like the roll result, so every card on a phone opens the same way. It closes with the × at the top, like the roll result, so Close never scrolls out of reach.

6Prone opens inside the card

Tapping Prone opens its rule right under the word; tapping it again closes it. One level only. Long text scrolls inside the sheet.

7After a hit, the roll result offers it

A player who never read Topple is told when it applies, with the numbers filled in. Prone opens here the same way.

8Desktop

Desktop has the room, so the card opens beside the band, here with Prone open.

9Fixed: the app's rule card on a phone

Not a mockup. The card the app shows for a condition (here "charmed" in Charm Person) ran 12px past the right edge of a phone screen. Fixed on this branch; it now keeps 16px from both edges.

10Rest controls with dawn

On the Features tab, beside the rest buttons. "Dawn passed" sits by Finish Long Rest, checked by default; unchecked, once-per-dawn uses stay spent. "Dawn" resets only dawn uses, for a dawn without a rest. Both show only for a character holding something that recharges at dawn.

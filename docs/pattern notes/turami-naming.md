## Turami Naming Rules

### Male Names
- **Structure**:  
  - Either a full name from `::male`, or  
  - Constructed from `::male-pre` + `::male-post`  
- **Examples**:  
  - `"Anton"` (from list)  
  - `"Marcon"` (from list)  
  - `"Sanenzo"` (`San` + `enzo`)  
  - `"Gianbero"` (`Gian` + `bero`)

### Female Names
- **Structure**:  
  - Either a full name from `::female`, or  
  - Constructed from `::female-pre` + `::female-post`  
- **Examples**:  
  - `"Luisa"` (from list)  
  - `"Fianna"` (from list)  
  - `"Gisela"` (`Gise` + `la`)  
  - `"Almira"` (from list)

### Surnames
- **Structure**:  
  - Either a full name from `::surname`, or  
  - Constructed from `::surname-pre` + `::surname-post`  
- **Examples**:  
  - `"Etxeberri"` (from list)  
  - `"Zubiondo"` (from list)  
  - `"Arriaga"` (from list)  
  - `"Mariondo"` (`Mari` + `ondo`)  
  - `"Delgato"` (from list, corrected to `"Delgado"` if needed)

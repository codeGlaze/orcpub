Natural Armor — one effect row, two tiers

Read the three boxes below as ONE row in three states, not three boxes on screen. An author adding Natural Armor gets a single row. A second row appears only for the genuine "whichever is better" case, where two calculations compete and the max wins.

The template is the shape these share. The differences between published races are knob values, not separate templates — except where a race adds a second fact, which gets its own control rather than being folded into the number.

Start from

A preset FILLS the row and then gets out of the way — every field stays editable and the stored :props are the same either way. It does not watch the number and fire on its own: 13 means lizardfolk in one race and a flat shell in another, so a form that rewrites fields the author did not touch is a form the author stops trusting.

Shields

A shield is never authored here, and never a rider. Calculations COMPETE (?ac-fns takes the max); the shield's bonus and magic bonuses are summed onto whichever one wins. So a tortle keeps the shield bonus on top of 17 without the form saying anything about shields.

The one time a shield is the author's business is disqualification: the advanced tier's Shield: only while NOT wielding drops the whole calculation while one is held — that is Unarmored Defense, not a skipped +2. Storage is :shield? false, and absent is not the same as false: absent means "does not care".

Why the toggle is separate

“Your AC is 17” and “your shell replaces armor” are two different facts. The old :tortle-ac prop welded them together, which is why a tortle could not be given armor-wearing homebrew without losing the AC too. The AC refactor split them; this form keeps them split.

Presets: picked, not sprung

Two ways were on the table: values that apply themselves when the AC number hits a known figure (overridable), or templates the author picks. Picked wins. A form that edits fields the author did not touch cannot be trusted afterwards, one number does not identify one race (13 + Dex and a flat 13 are different races), and a preset that fires on a value has to decide what to do when the author then changes it back. Picked templates cost one click, carry the sentence a number alone does not, and are the same template tier already decided for the feat widgets (builder-disposition-audit.md §REFRAMING) — a template is a parameter sheet over the same :props, never a second storage shape.

What the advanced tier is for

Three fields, collapsed by default via optional-builder-section — it opens automatically when the data already has them and exports nothing if untouched. The common case stays two knobs and a toggle; the whole formula is one click away.

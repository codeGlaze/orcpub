What changed for the author

  • Ten hardcoded widgets → one Grants card. Languages, skill/tool, weapon, armor, resistance, ancestry — every "gain X" is the same row shape.
  • Fixed vs choice is one toggle, not two different widgets living in two silos.
  • Numbers are numbers. +1…+5 initiative and 5/10/15 speed were checkbox rows; now an Effects kind with a number field. The compiler never had the range.
  • AC vocabulary reaches feat. Natural Armor and Armor Dex Cap were authorable only in fighting styles; the compiler accepted them all along.
  • Nothing to relearn between feat and race: race is the same cards minus Prerequisites and Spellcasting.

What did not change

  • Header, prerequisites, spellcasting templates — bespoke or shared as before.
  • Every legacy key still compiles. A pack from today imports, normalizes, and renders here as grant rows.

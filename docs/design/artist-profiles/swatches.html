The pairs

Every colour is at least 4.3:1 on its own background (icons need 3:1). The dark versions alone fail on white; yellow is 1.4:1. That’s why each colour is a pair.

One thing about the yellow

  • It’s the site’s warning-yellow (#ffd21a), which also marks unresolved conflicts and missing fields in the library health bar. As a small icon in a credit it doesn’t read as a warning, but if the health bar ever sits near a credit, the two could be confused.

Site mark picker

The artist picks an icon, one of five colours, and how that colour looks in the light theme: a deeper shade of itself or a paired colour. That covers both sets from last round without showing ten swatches. Drawn inside the running site in place of the profile card; the preview is the real credit.

What the account stores

  • An icon name, a colour name and a light-theme mode. The site turns those into the dark or light colour to match each visitor.
  • In the dark theme the two modes look the same. The mode only changes what light-theme visitors see, which is why it’s a second, smaller choice.

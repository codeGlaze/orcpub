How the artist’s choice would work

  • Each link in the registry already has its own icon and colour fields. That’s how Twitch gets its purple. Letting the artist pick just means those two fields come from their account, limited to an approved list.
  • Icons for their own site: browser window (the default if they never pick), house, compass, sparkle.
  • Colours: a short list checked against both themes, starting with coral and teal. A free colour picker could land on something unreadable on one of the backgrounds.
  • Twitch, Bluesky, Ko-fi and the rest keep their own brand mark and colour. Only the artist’s own site link is theirs to style.

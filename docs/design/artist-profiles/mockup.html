The page as built today, a recommended Carrd-style version, and the everything version, followed by the four open questions from the last round. Anything not named Fusspot is placeholder data.

1. How much of a Carrd should it be?

You're right that artists will expect to link whatever they like. My suggestion is to let them link anything, but keep the page itself the site's own: their words and links in our layout, rather than a page they design. The tabs show the three levels.

Recommended

    OptionCallWhy
    Links to anything on the webYesAny http(s) address, in the artist's order. Known services get their mark; the rest get a letter badge and the site's name.
    How many linksUp to 8That's enough for a site, a stream, socials, a shop and a tip jar. Past that, the page becomes a list nobody reads, and the featured link stops standing out.
    One featured linkYesShould be the same link as the credit's name link, so the artist sets where people go in one place.
    Show the destination under each linkYesVisitors can see where they're going. Cheap protection if an account is ever taken over.
    Short bioYesPlain text, 280 characters. No formatting means nothing to sanitise.
    Commission statusYesOpen, closed or waitlist. It's the thing people most want to know, and it doesn't take a link.
    Artist picks the showcase portraitsYesThree pinned portraits, built in the compositor with their pieces. Seeded examples remain the fallback.
    Email, phone, link shortenersNoYou said no email on the page. Shorteners hide where a link goes.
    Uploaded images (banner, gallery)NoWe'd be hosting and moderating other art. Their site already does that.
    Custom colours and fontsNoThe page is ours, and it has to read in both themes. Their own site is where they style things.
    Embedded playersLater, maybeThe site's security policy blocks third-party frames. A plain "Live now" badge could come later.
    Guestbook or commentsNoIt needs moderation and gives a way to harass someone.

    2. Where the profile link goes in a credit

    The name always goes to the artist's own link. These are the options for a second, quieter link to the page. The drawer version is already built.

    Drawer Built

    Art by
    Fusspot
    About the artist ›

    Matches the ART BY label, so it reads as part of the credit, not as another name.

    Character page · as now

    Art: Fusspot

    No way to reach the page from here.

    Character page · second line Suggest

    Art: FusspotAbout the artist ›

    Same wording as the drawer, one extra line of 7.5px caps. Readers see this credit more than any other.

    Character page · small mark

    Art: Fusspoti

    No height added, but a lone circled “i” is easy to miss and hard to tap on a phone.

    3. Piece tiles

    Every piece is drawn in the full portrait frame, so small pieces are specks in their tiles. Cropping each tile to the piece fixes that.

    Full frame · as now

    Faithful to the art, but the nose and mouth tiles look empty.

    Cropped to the piece Suggest

    Needs a bounding box per piece: measured once when the art is imported and stored in the registry, so the page never has to load images to measure them.

    4. Who picks the example portraits

    Seeded · as now

    Automatic and stable, but the colours are whatever the seed picks.

    Artist pins three Suggest

    Built in the portrait compositor they already know, then pinned to the page. Falls back to seeded examples until they pin any.

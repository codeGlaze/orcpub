# Homebrew Builders — Issue Deep Dive

> 75 issues (18 bugs, 57 enhancements) | [Back to Index](INDEX.md)

## Summary

The Homebrew Builders domain is the largest issue category in OrcPub, reflecting that homebrew content creation is the app's core differentiator. Issues cluster around three themes: (1) the builder UIs lack many modifier types and selection options that D&D 5e content requires (AC, darkvision, saving throws, spell attack bonuses), creating a "modifier gap" that prevents users from faithfully reproducing non-SRD content; (2) the selection/nesting system is fragile, with multiple bugs around selections not nesting, persisting after changes, or breaking in specific builder contexts; and (3) specific D&D content (Artificer, Draconic Sorcerer, fighter maneuvers, half-elf variants) is missing or incorrect in the hardcoded SRD templates.

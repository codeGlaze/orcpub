About the cloaking — please read

Glaze and Nightshade are real research tools from the University of Chicago. Glaze adds perturbations that protect an artist's style from being mimicked; Nightshade "poisons" images so models that train on them learn wrong concept associations.

The real tools depend on trained neural networks and meaningful compute, and cannot run as a small in-browser script. The options here are approximations: structured, perceptually-masked adversarial perturbations in the spatial domain. They can degrade naive scraping and feature extraction and add a real cost to casual misuse, but they are not equivalent to, and should not be relied on as a substitute for, the official tools. For serious protection use the official Glaze/Nightshade releases.

This tool processes everything locally; your images never leave the page.
